```
julia> vcat(df1, df2, cols=:intersect)
4×1 DataFrame
 Row │ a
     │ Int64
─────┼───────

```
julia> vcat(df1, df2)
ERROR: ArgumentError: column(s) c are missing from argument(s) 1, and column(s) b are missing from argument(s) 2
